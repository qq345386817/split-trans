Common questions

Is PhraseCut only a translator?

No. Translation is part of the workflow, but PhraseCut is organized around learning: context, saved study items, review, and export.

What can I save?

You can save words, phrases, and full sentences with the sentence context and translation.

Can I export my study materials?

Yes. The Library can export CSV or Anki-compatible CSV files for your own workflow.

Learn words from articles that match your interests

Explore short excerpts about travel, technology, science, culture, and other interests from public sources that keep updating. Translate a sentence and its words, then practice listening with adjustable speed and repeats. Each suggestion links to its original article and license; it is not a complete news article or an audio recording by the publisher.

Wikipedia · Global Voices · NASA · Wikivoyage

Recommendations currently use English-language sources. You can also paste your own text in any of the 20 supported translation languages. Browser pronunciation depends on the voices installed on your device. This is a vocabulary and listening practice tool, not a multilingual news feed or an AI tutor.
