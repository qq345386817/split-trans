Questions about online sentence learning

Is the online sentence translator free?

Yes. The web tool needs no account. It uses Google machine translation for the full sentence and individual words, with a limit of 400 characters and 40 words per analysis. Availability and rate limits still apply.

Can I export vocabulary from a sentence to Anki?

Yes. Select the words you want and export Anki CSV. Import the file into Anki and map Word and Meaning to your front and back fields. Sentence, Translation, Source language, and Target language retain the original context. This is a CSV file, not an APKG deck.

Does each word translation describe its exact role in the sentence?

Words are translated separately. A word with several meanings or a grammatical function may need the full sentence to interpret it correctly. Read the sentence translation alongside the word breakdown; these are machine translations, not AI tutor explanations.

Can I save sentences or use offline translation on the website?

The website has no saved history or review library and needs an internet connection for translation. The iPhone, iPad, and Mac app keeps a local study library, supports spaced repetition, and uses Apple Translation offline after the required language packs are downloaded.
