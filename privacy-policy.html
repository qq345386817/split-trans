Privacy

PhraseCut Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how PhraseCut and the companion website at phrase-cut.luopeike.com handle information.

App data

PhraseCut is designed to keep your study data on your device. Saved items, review data, and app settings are stored locally unless you export them yourself.

Personal information

You do not need to create an account to use the app. We do not directly collect your name, address, or similar personal profile information through the app.

Analytics and tracking

This website does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and the app does not build advertising profiles about you.

Exports and user actions

If you export your library to CSV or Anki-compatible files, that action is initiated by you. The resulting file is handled by the destination you choose.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact cameoshell09@gmail.com.

Online sentence learning

When you analyze a sentence on this website, its text and selected languages are sent from your browser to Google Translate. If a direct network connection fails, our Cloudflare Worker may relay the request. Google handles these requests under its own privacy policy. We do not write the text or results to a database or keep a study history. Google and Cloudflare process network information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect their services. IP addresses are used for temporary rate limiting. Export files are created in your browser. Pronunciation uses your browser or operating system's voices and may use the voice provider's online service. Avoid submitting personal or sensitive information.
